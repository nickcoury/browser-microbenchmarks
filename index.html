Methodology

  • Each benchmark runs a warmup round to JIT-stabilize.
  • Measurements use performance.now() with sub-millisecond precision.
  • Outliers beyond 2σ are discarded automatically.
  • Results show ops/sec (higher is better) and relative comparison to the fastest variant.
